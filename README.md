## 🚀 Key Features

* **Indian Rupee (₹) Pricing**:
  * Clean price tags (e.g. `₹349`, `₹1,499`) without extra prefixes.
* **Brand Colors & Playful Styling**:
  * Centered around `#fed41e` and `#ec4d2d` with smooth gradients, warm glowing card shadows, and floating toy shapes.
  * Dual typography: `Fredoka` for cheerful headings and `Quicksand` for crisp readability.
* **Interactive Category Filter Tabs**:
  * Easily toggle between *All 20 Toys*, *Vehicles & Wheels (10)*, *Dolls & Houses (3)*, *Plush & Soft (2)*, *Pretend Play Sets (4)*, and *Building Blocks (1)*.
* **Quick View Modal & One-Click Inquiry**:
  * Click **"Quick View"** on any toy to inspect features, piece counts, and safety specifications.
  * Click **"Inquire / Reserve This Toy"** to auto-fill the contact form with that toy's name and scroll straight to it.
* **Live Store Hours & Status**:
  * Calculates real-time **Open Now** or **Closed Now** status and highlights today's schedule in the hours table.
* **Toy Inquiry & Reserve Form**:
  * Live validation, reservation hold options, and festive confetti explosion in `#fed41e` and `#ec4d2d`.
